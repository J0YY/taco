# 13 Final Certification Claim

## Voiceover

The final claim is deliberately narrow.

SAE features, DreamAudit replay, and a runtime monitor do not prove that every
feature is causal. They do support a practical certification workflow: identify
a failure mode from internals, replay it, test whether the warning arrives early,
and issue a conditional readiness tier.

That is the interpretability-side story TACO is trying to make concrete.

## Visual Beats

- SAE features, replay evidence, and runtime monitor combine into a readiness
  tier.
- Final caution states the limitation and the certification claim.
