# 08 Replay Behavior Versus Internal Timing

## Voiceover

A replay tells us what happened. Internals can tell us when the risk started.

The same camera frame and instruction flow through the policy. The visible
trajectory may look fine for a while. But the residual stream can already be
moving toward a risky state.

This is why TACO looks at both: the external failure replay and the internal
activation trace.
