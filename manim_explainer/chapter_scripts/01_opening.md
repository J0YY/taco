# 01 Opening Claim

## Voiceover

TACO is not trying to certify a robot from the outside only. A replay can show
that a robot failed, but certification needs a more precise question: did the
model give us an internal warning before the failure became visible?

Here are the concrete signals we use in this explainer: a sparse autoencoder
trained on policy activations, a small number of active features on each step,
and a real FR-004 monitor that fired many steps before failure.

The goal is to turn those signals into pre-deployment certification evidence.

## Visual Beats

- Four metric cards introduce the local SAE and FR-004 evidence.
- The source line establishes whether numbers came from local artifacts.
- The chapter frames the rest of the animation as certification evidence.
