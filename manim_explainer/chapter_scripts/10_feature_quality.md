# 10 General Versus Memorized Features

## Voiceover

Not every feature is certification-grade.

A general feature fires across related scenes and lines up with behavior. That
kind of feature can become a monitor candidate.

A memorized feature may fire sharply in one replay and disappear under a small
perturbation. That is still useful evidence, but it is not enough by itself for
a readiness tier.
